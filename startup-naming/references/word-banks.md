# Word banks

Starting points, not limits. Pull from the territories chosen in step 3, respect the session-retired list and the rotation rule (no first word or participle more than 3 times in a round), and skip any word the competitor scan put on the exclusion list.

- **Multiples & numbers:** Tenfold, Threefold, Trifold, Sevenfold, Ninefold, Hundredfold, Manifold, Compound
- **Founding & commitment:** Founding, Founder, Held, Kept, Bound, Chosen
- **Time & patience:** Slow, Patient, Gradual, Measured, Perennial, Elder, Season, Ripened, Long, Still, Quiet
- **Human condition:** Effort, Toil, Labour, Grit, Struggle, Hunger, Necessity, Habit, Practice, Craft, Will, Nerve, Intent, Discipline, Silence, Solitude, Brink, Edge
- **Earth & growth:** Taproot, Root, Seed, Sapling, Canopy, Heartwood, Timber, Grain, Loam, Ridge, Plateau, Upland, Treeline, Cairn, Trail, Glacier, Moraine, Quarry, Vein, Lode, Seam, Furrow, Harvest, Orchard, Grove
- **Sea & navigation:** Tide, Keel, Swell, Breaker, Spindrift, Undertow, Groundswell, Windward, Ballast, Bowline, Halyard, Rudder, Mooring, Fathom
- **Force & pressure:** Pressure, Depth, Friction, Surge, Anvil
- **Light & sky:** Daybreak, Torch, Lantern, Dawn, Morrow, Zenith, Horizon
- **Direction:** Onward, Further, Greater, Upward, Outward, Higher
- **Abstract second words:** Arc, Order, Canon, Tenet, Axis, Frame, Course, Measure, Rise, Signal, Motion, Thesis, Mandate, Constant, Principle, Standard, Ground
- **Participles for compounds:** -born, -formed, -bred, -raised, -reared, -carved, -hewn, -wrought, -honed, -tempered, -quenched, -hardened, -distilled, -refined, -ripened, -matured, -tried, -tested, -proven, -taught, -earned, -won, -sown, -planted, -engraved, -kindled, -moulded, -settled, -steadied

## Before a word goes into a candidate

- **Religion pass early.** Some bank words sit close to sacred metaphors (Lantern, Dawn, Torch, -kindled, -tried, -refined next to "fire"). They are not banned, but the pairing decides it: "tried by fire" and "refiner's fire" are claimed. Run rule 6 on the pair, not the single word.
- **Pronunciation pass early.** -wrought and -hewn fail the phone test for many listeners; Halyard, Moraine and Spindrift need spelling out. Use them only when the meaning is worth that cost.
- **Mix the shapes.** Across 150–300 candidates, cover every shape the user ticked in question 5: compounds (Word + participle), two-word pairs (evocative word + abstract second word), and natural-sounding coinages if asked for.

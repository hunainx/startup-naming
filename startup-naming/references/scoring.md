# Scoring finalists

Score only names that have already passed the registry check, the searchability check, and the religion and pronunciation passes. Six criteria, each 1–5, total out of 30. The score orders the list and exposes weak spots; it never overrides a hard rule.

## The six criteria

| Criterion | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|-----------|---|---|---|
| **Meaning depth** | Three layers hold up: dictionary → business → life. A founder could give a two-minute talk on it. | Dictionary and business meaning fit; no life layer. | Meaning has to be explained or forced. |
| **Phone test** | Said once on a call, spelled right first time. 1–3 syllables per word. | Needs "that's X-with-a-Y" once. | Needs spelling out every time, or has a silent letter. |
| **Signboard test** | Alone on a building or a letterhead, it reads as a serious, lasting company. | Reads as a decent startup, not an institution. | Reads as a side project, a game or a gimmick. |
| **Searchability** | Take the score from `searchability.md` (qualifier count). | | |
| **Architecture test** | NAME Pay / NAME Ventures / "a NAME company" under each existing sub-brand all sound natural. | Works for some extensions, awkward for others. | Boxes the company into one product or market. |
| **Pronunciation (India / UK / US)** | All three say it the same way without being taught. | Two of three agree; the third drifts on one vowel or stress. | Three different readings, or a reading that means something unfortunate. |

Score 2 and 4 when a name sits between the anchors.

## Rules

- **Any 1 is a cut**, whatever the total. A 1 is a defect that will cost the company every day.
- **24–30:** headline name.
- **20–23:** show it, but put the low criterion in its ⚠ line.
- **Under 20:** leave it out of the headlines; it can appear in the grouped remainder only if the user asked for volume.

## How to test pronunciation

Write a plain respelling of how each listener would most likely say it, e.g. `Tidecarved — IN: TIDE-karvd · UK: TIDE-kahvd · US: TIDE-karvd`. The non-rhotic UK "r" alone is not a drift; a changed vowel, a moved stress or an added syllable is.

## Ties

When two finalists are within two points, prefer in this order: the deeper meaning, then the cleaner phone test, then the better searchability. Meaning is the one thing that can't be added later.
